Cookie Policy

How cookies are used on this platform.

This policy explains what cookies are, how EduPlex uses them, and how you can control them. By continuing to use the platform you consent to the use of cookies as described here. You can change your preferences at any time through your browser settings.

What are cookies?

Cookies are small text files placed on your device when you visit a website. They are widely used to make sites work efficiently, to remember preferences, and to provide information to site owners and third parties.

How we use cookies

We use a small number of cookies to keep the platform functioning smoothly and to remember basic preferences such as interface state. We may also use analytics cookies to understand, in aggregate, how the library is used so that we can improve it. These cookies do not identify you personally.

Third-party and advertising cookies

This site may display advertising provided by third-party networks, which can set their own cookies to deliver and measure advertisements. These providers operate under their own privacy policies. We do not share directly identifying information with them.

Managing cookies

Most browsers allow you to view, manage and delete cookies through their settings. You can choose to block cookies or to be notified when one is set. Please note that disabling certain cookies may affect how parts of the platform function. For more information about how we handle data, see our Privacy Policy.
