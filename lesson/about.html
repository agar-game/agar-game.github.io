About EduPlex

Our mission and how this library is maintained.

EduPlex is an open repository of interactive academic modules created to support active, self-directed learning. We believe that the most effective study happens when learners engage directly with a problem, experiment with possible solutions, and learn from immediate feedback. Every resource in our catalogue is selected and presented with that principle in mind.

What we offer

Our library brings together a wide range of reasoning and problem-solving activities in a single, uncluttered environment. Each module is designed to be approachable within minutes yet rich enough to reward repeated practice. The interface is deliberately simple so that learners can spend their time thinking, not navigating.

Who we serve

EduPlex is used by students strengthening their analytical skills, by educators looking for engaging warm-up activities, and by independent learners who simply enjoy a well-designed challenge. No registration is required and no personal information is needed to begin — the resources are open and immediately usable on any modern device.

Our commitment

We are committed to keeping the platform lightweight, fast and accessible, including on the modest connections common in schools and libraries. The collection is reviewed and improved continually, guided by feedback from the learners and educators who rely on it. If you would like to share a suggestion, please visit our contact page.
