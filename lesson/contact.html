Contact

We are glad to hear from learners and educators.

Whether you have a question about how the platform works, a suggestion for improving a resource, or a content enquiry, we welcome your message. Feedback from the community directly shapes how the library evolves.

General enquiries

For questions about using EduPlex or general feedback, please email contact@mathlesson10.help. We aim to respond to all reasonable enquiries within a few business days.

Feedback

Tell us which modules you found most useful, what you would like to see added, or any difficulty you encountered. Practical feedback of this kind is invaluable and helps us keep the platform fast, simple and genuinely useful for study.
