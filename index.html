Hardware & Network Tuning

School Chromebook 60 FPS Tuning Guide (Stop Split Stutter)

School-issued Intel Celeron and MediaTek Chromebooks throttle hard when Chrome runs 15 tabs at once. Follow these four tweaks before you spawn into agar-game.github.io for locked 60 FPS frame pacing:

  • 1. Lock Browser Zoom at 100% Press Ctrl + 0 before spawning. Running HTML5 Canvas at 90% or 110% browser zoom forces Chrome's compositor to rescale every frame on the CPU, cutting FPS by up to 30%.
  • 2. Use True Fullscreen (F4 / F11) Hit the fullscreen key on the top row of your Chromebook keyboard. Fullscreen mode disables the Chrome bookmark bar and tab strip redraws so the canvas gets direct GPU layer priority.
  • 3. Kill Background Google Meet / Docs Tabs A single open Google Docs or Slides tab eats 400MB+ of RAM on a 4GB Chromebook. Close heavy tabs so garbage collection spikes don't freeze your screen mid-split.
  • 4. Plug In a USB Mouse If You Can Chromebook trackpads apply palm-rejection delay right after you hit Spacebar or W. A basic wired mouse eliminates cursor freeze when rapid-firing 7 virus shots.

How do I shoot a green virus into another player?

Position your cell so the green spiky virus sits directly between you and your target. Aim your mouse cursor through the center of the virus toward the enemy cell and press W seven times fast. Each W adds mass to the virus; on the 7th shot, the virus splits and fires a new spiky virus forward, popping the enemy into up to 16 tiny edible pieces.

How much mass do I need so my split actually eats someone?

Your split half must be at least 25% to 33% larger than the target cell to swallow it on contact. How long does it take for my cells to merge back together after splitting?
