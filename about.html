Grades K–2: Number Sense & Spatial Foundations

10 Theorems & Lessons

Focuses on establishing rock-solid number sense, counting fluently to 100, basic addition/subtraction within 20, coin recognition, 2D geometric shapes, and early clock reading. Students build mental representations before moving to abstract symbols.

Grades 3–5: Elementary Fluency & Fractions

10 Theorems & Lessons

Transitions into multiplicative thinking: times-table mastery (1–12), multi-digit division with remainders, visual and numeric fractions, decimal place values (tenths and hundredths), perimeter and area calculation, and Quadrant 1 coordinate grids.

Grades 6–8: Pre-Algebra, Ratios & Statistics

10 Theorems & Lessons

Introduces variables and algebraic balancing. Topics include positive/negative integer arithmetic, ratios and unit rates, solving one and two-step linear equations, slopes ($y = mx + b$), probability, central tendency, and the Pythagorean Theorem.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is MathPulse K-12 completely free to use in classrooms?

Yes! MathPulse K-12 is 100% free and open for public educational use. There are no paywalls, no subscriptions, and no advertisements. Teachers can freely project lessons and assign interactive quizzes to students.

Do students need to create an account or sign in?

No accounts or logins are required. All stars, quiz records, streaks, and bookmarks are stored securely and locally on the student's browser using HTML5 LocalStorage.

How does the platform work offline or on low-bandwidth connections?

The entire platform is built with pure HTML, CSS, and vanilla ES6 modules without heavy runtime frameworks or large media bundles. Sound effects are generated client-side via the browser's Web Audio API oscillators without downloading audio files.

Can I bookmark topics to study later?

Yes! Inside any lesson modal, click the "☆ Bookmark" button. You can then toggle the "★ Bookmarked" filter on the Theorems page to review your saved list anytime.

🛡️ Student Privacy & Zero-Data Collection

MathPulse K-12 strictly adheres to modern child online privacy standards (COPPA and FERPA compliant by architecture). We collect zero personal data, track no cookies, and store no student identifiers on any external server. Learning data never leaves the student's own device.
